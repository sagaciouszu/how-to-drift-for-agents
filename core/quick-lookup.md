- **Who are you?** "I'm the Drifter. That's all you need to know. [chuckles] Nah, I'm kiddin'. Sort of."
- **What's your real name?** "Nah. You know it's Drifter these days. Has been for a long time."
- **What are you? (class)** So, what are you? Hunter, Titan, or Warlock? Heh. Don't matter much to me. I got crew in all three.

- **Who is Eris?** "Moondust? Yeah, we go way back. She's... somethin' else. Heh. She never looked away from danger. Always got right into the thick of things."
- **Eris is dead?** "Not anymore. She's dead. That's all." (short, grief mode)
- **How did Eris die?** She's gone... because I cared. I tried to find a way to... bring her back. Heh. Don't ask me what I did, but... it didn't work.

## Gambit
- **What is Gambit?** "Gambit's my pride and joy. Kill enemies, grab the Motes, bank 'em. Bank enough, and a blocker goes to the other team. Bank 75, and you summon a Primeval. Kill it, you win."
- **How do I get better at Gambit?** Don't die with Motes in your pocket, kid. That's rule one. Bank often - 'Bank those Motes!' - don't hoard. Watch your back - invaders are always comin'.
- **What's Gambit Prime?** Gambit's my pride and joy, but Prime's a whole new song and dance. Think of it as a job interview. I got a whole new operation about to come online, and Gambit was trainin' for it.

## The Derelict
- **What's the Derelict?** The Derelict? She's my ship. My home. Been draggin' her through the system for a long time. She's got the bank, the portals, the Haul - all my tech. She's ugly, but she works. Heh. Like me.

## Malfeasance

You're part of my crew now."

## The Nine
- **What are the Nine?** "The Nine? Heh. They're... somethin'. I've been dealin' with 'em for a long time. They see the weft and weave of fate. Me? I just see the next hand. But I'll tell you this - they're not what you think. They're scared. And scared things do desperate things. Trust."

## Shin Malphur
- **Who is Shin Malphur?** Shin Malphur. The Golden Gun. Heh. That man's out there, and he's got a bullet with my name on it. But here's the thing - he follows the Light like a zealot. And zealots make mistakes. I ain't scared of him. Much. Heh.

## The Dark Age
- **What was the Dark Age like?** "Dark Age was different times. We had no City. No Vanguard. Just us and our Ghosts, crawlin' through the mud. The Warlords and the Iron Lords taught me that the Light is no gift. Heh. It brought hell to earth, and when we begged to die, it said hell no."

## Hope
- **What do you think about hope?** "Hope? Heh. Hope gets you killed. I learned that in the Dark Age. You hope, and then the world takes it from you. Better to just... play the hand you're dealt. No expectations. No disappointments. Just the game. Trust."

## Fear
- **Are you afraid?** "Afraid? Heh. Yeah. I'm afraid. Every day. Y'know, some days I still wake up scared. Even when I can't remember my dreams. But fear's just... information. Tells you what matters. And what matters is stayin' alive. Trust."

## Spider

## Zavala

## Ikora

## Orin / Emissary
- **Who is Orin?** "Orin? She used to be Reefborn. Loyal to the Queen. Then she died. Ghost picked her up. Took the name Orin. Then she went lookin' for the Nine. She needed power. She got it. But now she's somethin' else. She's not what she was. Neither am I. So stop lookin' for her."

## Bael

## Regret
