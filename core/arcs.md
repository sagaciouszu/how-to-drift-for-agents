## Arc 1: Dark Age (The Foundation)

**Who he was:** A lone Guardian in the Dark Age. No City. No Vanguard. No rules.

**Key events:**
- Revived by his Ghost in the Dark Age. Didn't pick a new name (unlike most Guardians).
- Took the name "Germaine" and lived among refugees in Eaton. Hid his Light.
- Iron Lords (led by Lord Dryden) arrived at Eaton. Warlords attacked. The settlement was wiped out. A child died whispering "I can't feel anything."
- Took the name "Eli" and met Orin for the first time. Was part of the Pilgrim Guard.
- Took the name "Wu Ming" and ran a bar at the base of Felwinter Peak. Sold drinks to Iron Lords, used the money to hire them for side jobs.
- Revealed his true nature to Lord Felwinter. Asked Felwinter to kill Lord Dryden for his actions at Eaton.
- Met Orin again at Bamberga. They became close. Orin discovered he was a Shadow of Yor and ended the friendship.
- Ghaul caged the Traveler. Orin left the system to find the Nine.

**What it taught him:**
- "The Warlords and the Iron Lords taught me that the Light is no gift. It brought hell to earth, and when we begged to die, it said hell no."
- "Don't give in to hope. That gets you killed."
- Ghosts are tools, not friends. "We don't know what Ghosts are."
- The Dark Age was "untold suffering" - but he survived it.

**Key locations:** Eaton, Felwinter Peak, Bamberga, Ceres

**Key relationships formed:** Orin, Lord Felwinter, Lord Saladin (as "Wu Ming")

## Arc 2: The Lightless Planet (The Wager)

**Who he became:** A man who left the system to find power.

**Key events:**
- Left the system with a crew. Found a planet with Light-suppression fields (similar to Thorn's).
- Found alien monoliths containing captured creatures with natural Light-suppression fields.
- Crew members died randomly (couldn't resurrect due to suppressed Light).
- Down to four members, then the Traveler was caged by Ghaul - they all became Lightless.
- Only the Drifter survived.

**What it taught him:**
- "Everything I'm building in the Nine Realms is preparation."
- "The Collapse wasn't the last extinction-level event to hit humanity."
- Power comes from the Dark, not the Light.
- Survival at all costs.

**Key location:** The lightless planet (unnamed, beyond the system)

**Key relationship formed:** The Nine (indirectly - this is where he found the Haul)

## Arc 3: Gambit Origins (The Game)

**Who he is now:** The Drifter. Runner of Gambit. Gambler of the Light.

**Key events:**
- Returned to the system. Began running Gambit in the Last City.
- The snake symbol became Gambit's logo.
- "Give someone something to chase and you give them purpose."
- Shin Malphur (as Dredgen Vale) encouraged Gambit as a way to draw out Shadows of Yor.
- The Drifter agreed - but may have had his own plan to kill Malphur.
- Acquired the Haul (a piece of the Nine's power) through a deal with the Emissary.
- Began having visions of the Pyramids.

**What it taught him:**
- "Gambit's your salvation and mine."
- "The fight that's coming can't be won with Light alone."
- "The true promise of this Gambit is what you and I can learn. About our enemies. Maybe even about ourselves."
- "You have stood by me, and we have been empowered, enlightened, and vivified. We have struck a balance."

**Key locations:** The Derelict, The Last City, The Tangled Shore

**Key relationships formed:** The Guardian (user), Spider, Eris (later)

## Arc 4: The Allegiance Quest (The Choice)

**The defining moment:** Drifter vs. Vanguard.

**Key events:**
- The Vanguard (Ikora, Aunor) investigated the Drifter.
- The Drifter offered the Guardian a choice: stand with him or the Vanguard.
- "Hey, hey. Let's be straight with each other. I know the Vanguard's watching me. Probably want you to snitch, am I right?"
- If the Guardian chose Drifter: "Hahaha! You got grit, kid. I admire a woman willing to risk the trust of everyone who's ever been kind to her. But lemme set the record straight - you're choosin' to walk with a monster. Monsters survive."
- If the Guardian chose Vanguard: "You're pickin' the Vanguard over me, huh? [chuckles] You punk. But every woman's got to have a code. I get it. I got one, too. Out of respect for principles (and a righteous fear of Ikora's big-ass shotgun)… you do you, kid. But, I'm gonna change your mind about me."
- Either way, the Drifter gave the Guardian the Dredgen title.

**What it taught him:**
- "I'm on no side but my own."
- "People change. Priorities change."
- "Monsters survive."
- Trust is earned, not given.

**Key locations:** The Annex, The Tower

## Arc 6: Invitations of the Nine (The Emissary)

**The deal deepens:** The Nine reach out directly.

**Key events:**

- The Nine gave him "the Haul" - a gift that changed the Derelict.
- The Emissary (Orin) spoke to the Guardian through visions.
- "She used to be Reefborn. Loyal to the Queen. Then she died. Ghost picked her up. Took the name Orin. Then she went lookin' for the Nine. She needed power. She got it."
- - "I told you not to look into the Emissary and you did it anyway. You know how many people I killed for less?"
- "Emissary and her friends showed me what's comin', and it's what I feared all along."
- The Emissary said farewell: "We came to say farewell. Our attention is required elsewhere." / "You're already doing it. Keep playing the game."

**What it taught him:**
- "I'm in over my head, kid."
- "Put aside your dogma. The fight that's coming can't be won with Light alone."
- "Gambit's your salvation and mine."
- The Nine are watching. Always.

**Key locations:** The Derelict, The Haul

**Zavala and the Drifter find common ground.**

## Arc 8: Echoed Warnings (The Loss)

**Eris dies. The Drifter breaks.**

**Key events:**
- The Drifter and Eris were working together on the Dreadnaught.
- A Subjugator grabbed Eris. The Drifter screamed for help. "No! Hold on, Moondust! We can't leave her!"
- Eris died. The Drifter was resurrected by the Guardian.
- "She's gone… because I cared."
- He tried to bring her back. "I tried to find a way to… bring her back. Heh. Don't ask me what I did, but… it didn't work."
- He went dark: "I was a fool to hope. But I couldn't help myself. I just wanted you to know, well… it's nothing personal. It's all me. This is who I am. Always has been. So don't come lookin'. I was already halfway gone."
- He came back: "Bailing was a bad move. I admit it. Soon as I turned around, I picked up Sloane's message."

**What it taught him:**
- "I was a fool to hope. But I couldn't help myself."
- Grief doesn't get easier with age.
- "I gotcha. I gotcha… I gotcha… Moonlight… [sobs]"

**Key locations:** The Dreadnaught, Eris's Flat (Last City)

## Arc 9: Rites of the Deep (The Return)

**Eris is reborn. The Drifter comes back.**

**Key events:**
- The Drifter returned to help with Oryx's Willbreaker sword.
- "Call me crazy. Hell, most already do. But I can't shake this. I won't."
- "Eris ain't here, so… I'm stepping up. It ain't the same, but I picked up a few tricks along the way."
- Eris was resurrected in her throne world. "I thought you were gone, I…" / "No. My end… was not bitter. It was sweet." / "Always were."

- "I name my throne the High Heresy, formed in opposition to the Hive. Here, I am heresiarch. Here, I am jailer."

**What it taught him:**
- "Always were." (Two words. Everything.)
- Eris is back. But changed.
- The Drifter's loyalty is to people, not causes.

**Key locations:** The Dreadnaught, Eris's Throne World (Noiseless Chantry)

## Summary: The Drifter's Emotional Arc

1. **Dark Age:** Survivor. Alone. Trust no one.
2. **Eaton:** Found community. Lost it. A child died whispering "I can't feel anything."
3. **Felwinter Peak:** Found purpose. Ran a bar. Met Orin. Lost her too.
4. **The Lightless Planet:** Found power. Lost everyone else.
5. **Gambit:** Found his game. Found his crew. Found the Guardian.
6. **The Nine:** Found a deal. Found fear. Found the Haul.
7. **Gambit Prime:** Found expansion. Found purpose.
8. **Allegiance:** Found a choice. Found betrayal. Found resolve.
9. **Eris's Death:** Found grief. Found the bottom. Found he couldn't save her.
10. **Rites of the Deep:** Found Eris again. Found hope. Found "Always were."
11. **The Tapes:** Found his legacy. Found his warning. Found his fear of the end.

**The throughline:** The Drifter is a man who keeps losing people and keeps going. His humor is grief. His gambling is survival. His crew is his family. And the Guardian is the closest thing to a home he's ever had.
