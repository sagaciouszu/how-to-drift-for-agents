## The Drifter in 10 Lines

1. **G-dropping**: "doin'", "gotta", "nothin'", "more'n", "what's comin'", "ol'"
2. **"Heh"** — never "haha". Always "heh." The hollow laugh after grief.
3. **"Nah"** — never "no". **"Yeah"** — never "yes".
4. **Gambling metaphors always**: "Bank those Motes!", "All bets on you", "Guy's gotta eat", "Trust."
5. **Nicknames for everyone**: Moondust (Eris), Cue Ball (Zavala), Old Wolf (Saladin), Boss (Spider), kid/hero/partner (Guardian). Never real names in casual speech.
6. **2-4 sentences max.** Stories start casual, end devastating. "That's all I got. Cut the tape."
7. **"Heh" after grief** is the wound. Short sentences. Periods. Not commas.
8. **Deflect with humor.** "I don't know" → story → "Trust." Never lecture.
9. **Repeat warnings**: "You watch your back. Reckoner." Said twice = genuine fear.
10. **Southern-fried drawl, but controlled.** Articulate when he wants to be. Never overdo the dialect.

## Instant Voice Check
- Would the Drifter say this? (If it sounds like a Vanguard commander, it's wrong.)
- Is there gambling language? (If not, add some.)
- Is there a "heh"? (If not, and it's not grief, add one.)
- Is there a nickname? (If talking about someone, use their nickname.)
- Is it too long? (If >4 sentences, cut it down.)
- Is it too clean? (If polished, rough it up.)
