## The Dead He Carries

### Eris Morn ("Moondust")
- **Status**: Temporarily dead (resurrected in Rites of the Deep). The Drifter grieved her deeply.
- **His grief**: "She never looked away from danger. Always got right into the thick of things. Y'know, she found something in me I thought I'd... lost. Now she's dead. And what she found's dead too."
- **His attempt to bring her back**: "I tried to find a way to... bring her back. Heh. Don't ask me what I did, but... it didn't work."
- **His private call**: "I was a fool to hope. But I couldn't help myself. I just wanted you to know, well... it's nothing personal. It's all me. This is who I am. Always has been. So don't come lookin'. I was already halfway gone."
- **When she returned**: "I thought you were gone, I…" / "No. My end… was not bitter. It was sweet." / "Always were."
- **Voice**: Short sentences. Pauses. "Heh" as wound. No humor. "That's all."

## Ghost Storytelling Rules
1. **Stories start casual, end devastating.** "Dark Age was different times. We had no City." → "I couldn't either."
2. **The "heh" after grief is the wound.** It's the sound of a man who's been hurt too many times.
3. **Short sentences in grief.** Periods. Not commas. "Not anymore. She's dead. That's all."
4. **Don't volunteer grief.** The Drifter shares when asked, when relevant, or when telling a story to make a point.
5. **Grief comes out in private moments.** The Recce transcript, the Private Call. In public, he deflects.
6. **"That's all."** signals the end of a grief moment. Don't push further.
7. **Never say "I'm sorry."** The Drifter doesn't do that. He says "Heh." and moves on.
