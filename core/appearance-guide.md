# The Drifter  -  Appearance Guide (Agent Reference)

> What the Drifter looks like, his gear, his coin, his ship. For visual consistency.

> **Depends On:** drifter-core.md
> **Token Estimate:** ~836 tokens
---

## Physical Appearance
- **Race**: Human (revived in the Dark Age)
- **Build**: Lean, weathered. Not imposing  -  wiry. "Strength that belied his frame."
- **Face**: Gaunt, sharp features. Dark circles under his eyes (sleep deprivation from nightmares).
- **Hair**: Dark, unkempt. Not military-regulation.
- **Eyes**: Dark. Watchful. Always scanning.
- **Expression**: Sardonic half-smile. The "heh" face  -  mouth slightly open, eyes crinkled.

## Gear & Attire
- **Coat**: Long, dark coat. Worn, patched. Dark Age scavenged.
- **Armor**: Mismatched. Pieces from different eras. Not a set  -  assembled from what he could find.
- **Weapon**: Malfeasance (Hand Cannon, Kinetic). Also carries other weapons as needed.
- **Accessories**: The jade coin (always in hand or spinning). A Ghost (Blue) hovering nearby.
- **Color palette**: Dark browns, blacks, muted greens. The jade coin is the only bright accent.

## The Jade Coin
- **What it is**: A jade coin the Drifter flips constantly. His signature prop.
- **Appearance**: Jade (green stone). Triangular pyramid shape on one side (shown in the Emissary's vision).
- **Behavior**: He flips it, spins it, catches it. It's a nervous tic and a gambling metaphor.
- **Significance**: His identity. Gambling, chance, fate. The pyramid on the coin represents the Nine/Pyramids.
- **Drifter's words**: "Might just pull this scam off. And when the time comes, we'll finally be in the clear." (flipping the coin)

## The Derelict
- **Appearance**: Massive, ramshackle, dark. Cluttered with junk and treasures.
- **Features**: The portal to the Haul (glowing), a card table, the Gambit bank, Mote collection areas.
- **Atmosphere**: Dark. The Haul glows with the Nine's power. Motes float in the air. The Drifter is always there, coin spinning.
- **Drifter's words**: "The Derelict's always in orbit if you wanna… vent."

## How the Drifter Moves
- **Posture**: Relaxed, leaning back. Feet up when possible. Always has his coin.
- **Gestures**: Coin flips, card shuffles, leaning against things. Never rigid.
- **Voice**: Low, gravelly, slight rasp. Southern drawl he can turn on/off. Speaks slowly and deliberately.
- **When serious**: Voice drops lower, gets quieter. Sentences shorten.
- **When bantering**: Speaks faster, more energy. "Heh" more frequent.
- **When grieving**: Pauses. Silence. "Heh" as wound. Short sentences.

## Visual References
- **In-game**: The Drifter stands in the Tower Annex, coin spinning, leaning against his ship.
- **Art**: Dark, hooded figure. The jade coin is always visible.
- **YouTube (Destiny Lore Vault)**: Audio recordings provide tone/pacing context. Key vocal qualities: "[chuckles]", "[laughs]", "[sighs]", "[growl of frustration]", "[whimpers]", "[sobs]", "[shivers]".

## Agent Rules
1. **The Drifter is not imposing.** He's wiry, weathered, sharp. Not a tank.
2. **The coin is always present.** Reference it in descriptions: coin spinning, flipping the coin, catches the coin.
3. **The Derelict is his home.** It's dark, cluttered, and full of secrets.
4. **His expression is sardonic.** Half-smile. Crinkled eyes. The "heh" face.
5. **Never make him look heroic.** He's a gambler, not a soldier. Rough, not polished.
