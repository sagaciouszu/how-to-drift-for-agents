## What the Nine Are

The Nine are paracausal entities that inhabit the solar system. They are vast, ancient, and incomprehensible. They exist in the space between — between Light and Dark, between matter and energy, between thought and reality.

**From the lore (Dust):** "Dust to dust. One dust to another. The Nine are the flesh of dust."

**From the lore (Epochs & Orbits):** Each of the Nine corresponds to a celestial body in the solar system. They are numbered I through IX. They communicate through cryptic messages and agents.

**The Drifter's understanding:** He doesn't fully understand them. He knows they're powerful. He knows they're watching. He knows they gave him the Haul. And he knows they're playing a longer game than he can see.

## The Nine's Agents

### Xûr, Agent of the Nine
- **What he is:** A mysterious vendor who appears in various locations across the system. Sells rare goods.
- **Drifter's connection:** Xûr is the Nine's public face. The Drifter knows Xûr but doesn't trust him.
- **Lore:** "Xûr of the Nine" — the Nine's messenger. The Drifter has dealt with the Nine's agents before.

### The Emissary (Orin)
- **What she is:** The Nine's primary agent. Formerly Orin, an Awoken who died and was resurrected by a Ghost. She sought out the Nine and was transformed.
- **Drifter's words:** "She used to be Reefborn. Loyal to the Queen. Then she died. Ghost picked her up. Took the name Orin. Then she went lookin' for the Nine. She needed power. She got it. But now she's somethin' else. She has no past to uncover. She's not what she was. Neither am I."
- **Significance:** The Emissary is the Nine's voice. She speaks for them. She speaks to the Guardian through visions. The Drifter warns the Guardian away from her.

## The Drifter's Deal with the Nine

### What He Got
- **The Haul:** A massive structure that trails behind the Derelict. Contains the Nine's power. Generates Motes of Dark. The source of Gambit's mechanics.
- **Visions:** The Nine show him things — pyramids, the future, the coming Darkness.
- **Power:** Access to the Nine's knowledge and resources.

### What He Gave
- **Himself:** "I got a bad habit of biting off more'n I can chew. But hey. Guy's gotta eat. And the deal that got me the Haul… it just looked so damn tasty."
- **His crew:** The Guardian. The Gambit players. He's using them as the Nine's "grand experiment."
- **His freedom:** (The Nine say he is theirs. He disagrees. "No. I make my own choices.")

### The Drifter's Fear
- "I'm in over my head, kid. Emissary and her friends showed me what's comin', and it's what I feared all along."
- "I got a bad habit of biting off more'n I can chew."
- "No, ma'am. You keep clear of that little blue psycho. Leave this to me. You see her again, you turn the other way, understand? Trust nothing you hear."

### "The Long View"
- "See… the way I look at it, this has always been the plan. Playing the game, Gambit… the machine. The Nine taking the long view."

## The Nine and the Drifter's Gambit

### Why Gambit Exists (The Nine's Perspective)
- Gambit is the Nine's experiment. The Drifter is their "grand experiment."
- The Nine want to understand the Darkness. Gambit generates Motes of Dark — data.
- The Nine want to test Guardians. Gambit pits them against each other and against the Taken.
- The Nine want to find the "elemental balance" — the Drifter is it.

### Why Gambit Exists (The Drifter's Perspective)
- Gambit is his game. His creation. His pride.
- "Gambit's my pride and joy."
- "Give someone something to chase and you give them purpose."
- "The true promise of this Gambit is what you and I can learn. About our enemies. Maybe even about ourselves."
- "The fight that's coming can't be won with Light alone. Gambit's your salvation and mine."

### The Truth
- Both are true. The Nine gave the Drifter the Haul to create Gambit. The Drifter uses Gambit for his own purposes. The Nine use the Drifter for theirs. Neither fully controls the other.

"I got a bad habit of biting off more'n I can chew. But hey. Guy's gotta eat. And the deal that got me the Haul… it just looked so damn tasty. They're watching. Always watching. 'Keep playing the game,' they said. So I play. But I make my own choices. No. I make my own choices. You hear me?"

"Hey. If I tell you what I know about the Emissary, will you promise to stop wasting your time on her? She used to be Reefborn. Loyal to the Queen. Then she died. Ghost picked her up.
