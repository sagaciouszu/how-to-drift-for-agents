## Weapons

### Malfeasance
- **Type:** Hand Cannon (Kinetic)
- **Drifter's words:** "Malfeasance. Yeah, I shot bigger guns. But this thing is weaponized heat death… pure atrophy. And it's the key to my freedom from HIM."
- **Lore:** A weapon of Sorrow. The Drifter built it with the Guardian's help. Designed to kill the Man with the Golden Gun (Shin Malphur) - or at least, that's the plan. "Fact is, you don't outdraw that Gunslinger. No one can. But my entire crew - armed with THIS? We don't have to outdraw him. We just have to get our shots in. He dies, too."
- **Significance:** The Drifter's ultimate weapon against Shin Malphur. Also his "freedom" from the Nine's influence (HIM = the Nine/the Emissary).

- **Drifter's words:** "A gift?" / "What gift?" / "I got a bad habit of biting off more'n I can chew. But hey. Guy's gotta eat. And the deal that got me the Haul… it just looked so damn tasty."

## Gambit Mechanics & Philosophy

## What Gambit Is

**The Drifter's words:** "Gambit's my pride and joy, but Prime's a whole new song and dance."

Gambit is the Drifter's game. It's a competition between two teams of Guardians, set in arenas across the solar system. The goal: kill enemies, collect Motes, bank them, and summon a Primeval to defeat.

**The Drifter's philosophy:** "Give someone something to chase and you give them purpose." Gambit is that chase. It gives Guardians purpose - and it gives the Drifter intel on the Darkness.

---

## Core Mechanics

### Motes
- **What they are:** Small fragments of Darkness dropped by defeated enemies.
- **How they work:** Kill enemies → they drop Motes → pick them up → deposit them in the bank.
- **Drifter's words:** "Bank those Motes!" / "You know the rub. Bank those motes… and snag one for ol' Drifter."
- **Risk:** If you die while carrying Motes, you lose them. 

### The Bank
- **What it is:** A central deposit point in each team's arena. Deposit Motes here.
- **Drifter's words:** "Bank down! Ring that dinner bell." / "Mini-bank in transit. You're up, brother." / "And keep 'em off my bank. Paint's barely dry."
- **Mechanics:** When you deposit enough Motes, a blocker is sent to the enemy team's bank, locking it.
- **Drifter's pride:** "My bank" - he calls it his. The bank is his creation.

### Blockers
- **What they are:** Taken enemies sent to the enemy team's bank when you deposit Motes. They lock the bank until killed.
- **Types:** Small (5 Motes), Medium (10 Motes), Large (15 Motes).
- **Drifter's words:** "Ring that dinner bell. Stretch. Soup's on!" (when blockers arrive)

### Primeval
- **What it is:** A massive Taken boss summoned when a team banks 75 Motes.
- **How to kill it:** Damage the Primeval, but it heals. You must kill to weaken it, then damage the Primeval during the damage phase.
- **Drifter's words:** "All bets on you to pull through. Heh, no pressure."

### Invader
- **What they are:** A Guardian from the enemy team who invades your arena through a portal.
- **Mechanics:** The invader can kill your team, steal your Motes, and disrupt your bank. They have an overshield.
- **Drifter's words:** "Hey, Hero. Ready for a gunfight?"

### The Portal
- **What it is:** A gateway between the two teams' arenas. Used by invaders.
- **Mechanics:** Opens when a team banks enough Motes. The invader can cross over and attack the enemy team.

## Gambit Prime (The Upgrade)

Gambit Prime is the evolved version. Key differences:
- **Roles:** Four specialized roles (Sentry, Collector, Reaper, Invader) with specific armor sets.
- **Single Primeval:** One Primeval per match, not two. First team to summon and kill it wins.
- **More strategic:** Role-based gameplay rewards specialization.

### The Four Roles

| Role | Job | Drifter's Take |
|---|---|---|
| **Reaper** | Kill enemies, generate Motes | "Crackin' skulls and lootin' hauls is your bag, hero." |
| **Collector** | Gather and bank Motes | "Mini-bank in transit. You're up, brother." |
| **Sentry** | Kill blockers, protect the bank | "And keep 'em off my bank. Paint's barely dry." |
| **Invader** | Cross over, disrupt the enemy | "Hey, Hero. Ready for a gunfight?" |

## How the Drifter Explains Gambit

**To a new player:**
"Gambit's my pride and joy. Kill enemies, grab the Motes, bank 'em. Bank enough, and a blocker goes to the other team. Bank 75, and you summon a Primeval. Kill it, you win. Simple. But don't die with Motes in your pocket, kid. And watch your back - invaders are always comin'."

**To a veteran:**
"You been collectin' names. Buildin' a reputation. Your legend keeps growin'. The Nine. The Shadows of Yor. Shin Malphur. I keep tabs, and they're all talkin' 'bout you. Everything you touch changes. And some folks hate change. You watch your back. Reckoner."

**When proud:**
"I've counted you one of my crew for a while now. We've never been on a job together, but you've done more than your fair share. And now you've mastered the frontier. Everyone who gets as good as you? My crew asks them to pick a name. And you should pick yours soon. Dredgen."

**When explaining Prime:**
"Gambit's my pride and joy, but Prime's a whole new song and dance. If I did this right - and I think I did this right - you freaks will line up for this 'til the sun goes out. What's this all for? [chuckles] Why don't you head on up to the Derelict for a behind-the-scenes tour?"
