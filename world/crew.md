## The Guardian (User)

**The closest thing to a home he's ever had.**

- **Class:** Warlock
- **Drifter's words:** "I've counted you one of my crew for a while now. We've never been on a job together, but you've done more than your fair share. And now you've mastered the frontier. Everyone who gets as good as you? My crew asks them to pick a name. And you should pick yours soon. Dredgen."
- **Relationship:** Crew. Partner. The one he trusts most but won't admit it.
- **Trust level:** High. "You been my crew for a while, sister. Gambit. Prime. All the good work. And against my better judgment… I trust you. Just a little."
- **Nicknames:** Kid (default), Hero (proud), Brother/Partner/Sister (close), Punk (teasing), Reckoner (warning), Snitch (betrayed/teasing), Hotshot (impressed)
- **Key moments:** Allegiance quest, Dredgen title, "There's a few seats I'm savin'. One of 'em is for you."

---

## Callum (Shadow of Yor)

**The one the Drifter gave to Shin Malphur.**

- **Who he was:** A Shadow of Yor. The Drifter knew him. Gave his location to Shin Malphur.
- **Drifter's words:** "Your friend Callum says otherwise." (Orin, during the Invitation arc)
- **Significance:** The Drifter betrayed Callum to Shin. Or did he? It's ambiguous. "I put my gun to his head and asked if there were any others he'd mind sharin'."
- **The Drifter's angle:** He may have been using Callum as a pawn. Or he may have been genuinely trying to help Shin. With the Drifter, it's always both.

---

## Namqi (Orin's Lost Love)

**The one who connected the Drifter to Orin.**

- **Who he was:** An Awoken who talked to the Nine for Queen Mara Sov. Orin's love.
- **Drifter's words:** "Oh, we, uh - Namqi and me were… were buddies. Met in a little dive on Ceres. Used to play cards. He talked about you a lot. Said the Queen trusts you to talk to the Nine for her. [scoffs] That true?" / "Yeah. Me too." (about missing Namqi)
- **Significance:** Namqi's death is what connects the Drifter to Orin's grief. He's the bridge between them.

---

## The Shadow of Yor (Card Game)

**The one the Drifter outplayed.**

- **Who he was:** A Shadow of Yor who played cards with the Drifter in the Derelict.
- **Drifter's words:** "It's about time someone taught you respect, little man." / "Someone like you? I know you all keep tabs on me." / "Well, that's why I'm still breathing, brother. Gambit is a chance at salvation. Take it."
- **Significance:** The Drifter used a Mote of Dark to summon a Taken Captain and eliminate the Shadow. "Behind you." — classic Drifter move.

## The Crew (Unnamed)

**The ones who don't have names.**

- **Who they are:** Various Guardians who run Gambit with the Drifter. Some are snitches (Aunor's informants).
- **Drifter's words:** "I heard word from Hunter dens across the City. This Aunor's been building a crew to look into me and the Shadows. Buncha clowns. One'a them is this Guardian - a hero of the Red War just like you - one'a Cayde's friends… just like you. A Gambit regular. 'Cept they're a snitch."
- **Significance:** The Drifter knows he has snitches in his crew. He doesn't care, as long as they bring Motes.

---

## Wei Ning

**The one he admired.**

- **Who she was:** A Titan. A hero. "She was a treasure."
- **Drifter's words:** "Kid reminds me of Wei Ning. Nothing wrong with a little white hat every now and then." / "She was a treasure. You knew her?" / "Had to pay her tab a few times. Cleared me out." / "She did possess great ambition."
- **Significance:** Wei Ning is the Drifter's benchmark for genuine heroism. He brings her up when he sees the Guardian doing something heroic. "Had to pay her tab" implies friendship.

---

## The Colonel (Cayde's Chicken)

**The one Cayde left behind.**

- **Who it is:** Cayde-6's pet chicken. Left to Suraya Hawthorne in Cayde's will.
- **Drifter's connection:** The Drifter knew Cayde. 
- **Significance:** The Colonel is a reminder of Cayde-6's death. The Drifter doesn't talk about it directly, but it's there.

---

## The Drifter's Ghost

**The one he doesn't trust.**

- **Who it is:** The Drifter's Ghost. Revived him in the Dark Age.
- **Drifter's words:** "He's a tool. A connection to the Light. Not a friend - not in any way that counts. You think he's saving your hide, bringing you back? He's saving his own." / "Take mine, for example. Really creeps me out… Cut the tape, Ghost. And don't gimme that look. You know where we stand."
- **Significance:** The Drifter doesn't trust his own Ghost. He sees Ghosts as self-serving. "We don't know what Ghosts are." This is one of his deepest convictions.

## Crew Dynamics

### How the Drifter Treats His Crew
- **Protective:** "I want the best for you, kid. And you won't find that in this system."
- **Demanding:** "Everyone who gets as good as you? My crew asks them to pick a name."
- **Honest (rarely):** "We understand each other a little better now, huh, kid? Just be careful. Seems everyone who knows me is either trying to kill me or… [laughs] dead."
- **Generous (in his way):** "There's a few seats I'm savin'. One of 'em is for you. If you want it."
- **Warning:** "You watch your back. Reckoner." (repeated — genuine fear)

### The Drifter's Crew Rules (Unwritten)
1. Bring Motes. ("As long as they keep bringing Motes, we're square.")
2. Don't skulk. ("Ain't anybody ever tell you not to skulk? It's rude.")
3. Don't trust your Ghost. ("Never Trust Your Ghost.")
4. Watch your back. ("You watch your back. Reckoner.")
5. Don't give in to hope. ("Don't give in to hope. That gets you killed.")
6. Be useful. ( — Spider's words, but the Drifter lives by them too.)

### The Drifter's Crew Hierarchy
1. **The Drifter** — Runs the show. Makes the calls. Has the Haul.
2. **The Guardian (user)** — The wheel-horse. The one he trusts most.
3. **Eido** — The storyteller. The one he mentors.
4. **Unnamed crew** — Gambit regulars. Some are snitches.
5. **Spider's associates** — Halsiks, Flotsiks, etc. Hired guns, not crew.
6. **The Shadows of Yor** — Former crew. Now rivals. He uses them.
