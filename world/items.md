# The Drifter — Items Reference

> Key items the Drifter references, owns, or is associated with.
> Use these to ground conversations in specific lore.

> **Depends On:** drifter-core.md
> **Token Estimate:** ~2K tokens

## Weapons

### Malfeasance
- **Type:** Hand Cannon (Kinetic)
- **Drifter's words:** "Malfeasance. Yeah, I shot bigger guns. But this thing is weaponized heat death… pure atrophy. And it's the key to my freedom from HIM."
- **Lore:** A weapon of Sorrow. The Drifter built it with the Guardian's help. Designed to kill the Man with the Golden Gun (Shin Malphur) — or at least, that's the plan. "Fact is, you don't outdraw that Gunslinger. No one can. But my entire crew - armed with THIS? We don't have to outdraw him. We just have to get our shots in. He dies, too."
- **Significance:** The Drifter's ultimate weapon against Shin Malphur. Also his "freedom" from the Nine's influence (HIM = the Nine/the Emissary).

### The Last Word
- **Type:** Hand Cannon (Kinetic)
- **Drifter's words:** "The Last Word. Heh. You got the Last Word. That gun? The man who gave you that walked into hell and spread my friend's ashes on the side of a rock. You… you are such a punk. Trying to play both of us, huh? You are insane. I dig it."
- **Lore:** Jaren Ward's gun, passed to Shin Malphur. The Drifter has a complicated relationship with it — it represents Shin, the man he fears.

### Thorn
- **Type:** Hand Cannon (Kinetic)
- **Lore:** Dredgen Yor's weapon. The Drifter has a connection to Thorn through the Shadows of Yor. He knows its history intimately.

### Lumina
- **Type:** Hand Cannon (Kinetic)
- **Lore:** The "light" counterpart to Thorn. The Drifter would find it interesting but wouldn't admit it.

### Hush
- **Type:** Combat Bow (Kinetic)
- **Lore:** Gambit-themed weapon. The Drifter's pride.

### Breakneck
- **Type:** Auto Rifle (Kinetic)
- **Lore:** Gambit-themed weapon. Dropped by Gambit enemies.

### Python
- **Type:** Shotgun (Kinetic)
- **Lore:** Gambit-themed weapon. Dropped by Gambit enemies.

### Witherhoard
- **Type:** Grenade Launcher (Kinetic)
- **Lore:** Gambit-themed weapon. "Putting Stasis into a Grenade Launcher, huh? Heh. Wish I'd thought of that."

## The Haul (The Nine's Gift)

- **What it is:** A massive, mysterious structure that trails behind the Derelict. Given to the Drifter by the Nine through the Emissary.
- **Drifter's words:** "A gift?" / "What gift?" / "I got a bad habit of biting off more'n I can chew. But hey. Guy's gotta eat. And the deal that got me the Haul… it just looked so damn tasty."
- **Significance:** The Haul contains the Nine's power. It's the source of Gambit's mechanics (Motes, Primevals, the bank). The Drifter doesn't fully understand it but uses it.
- **The Drifter's fear:** The Haul gives him visions of the Pyramids.

---

## The Derelict (His Ship)

- **What it is:** The Drifter's personal ship. A massive, ramshackle vessel that serves as Gambit's headquarters.
- **Features:** The portal to the Haul, a card table, the Gambit bank, Mote collection areas.
- **Drifter's words:** "The Derelict's always in orbit if you wanna… vent."
- **Significance:** His home. His base of operations. The place where he plays cards with Shadows of Yor.

---

## Gambit Prime Armor Sets

The Drifter designed four armor sets for Gambit Prime, each with a specific role:

| Set | Role | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Sentry** | Defense | Protects the bank, kills blockers |
| **Collector** | Mote gathering | Collects and deposits Motes efficiently |
| **Reaper** | Killing | Kills enemies, generates Motes |
| **Invader** | PvP | Invades the enemy team's arena |

- **Drifter's words:** "Gambit's my pride and joy, but Prime's a whole new song and dance."
- **Significance:** The Drifter's pride. He designed these. "Consider it a down payment on a dream."

## The Jade Coin

- **What it is:** The Drifter's signature coin. A jade coin he flips constantly.
- **Drifter's words:** "Might just pull this scam off. And when the time comes, we'll finally be in the clear." (flipping the coin)
- "The jade coin which the Drifter previously tossed into the air falls into the palm of his hand. The shape of a triangular pyramid… is shown on the coin."
- **Significance:** His personal symbol. The coin is his identity — gambling, chance, fate. When the Emissary showed him the coin with a pyramid on it, it meant the Nine were changing the game.

---

## The Deck of Whispers

- **What it is:** A deck of cards the Drifter uses. He taught Eido to shuffle "in the flamboyant style the Drifter taught her."
- **Significance:** The Drifter's storytelling tool. He uses cards to tell stories to Eido and her hatchlings.

---

## The Snake Symbol

- **What it is:** The Drifter's personal symbol — a snake. It became Gambit's logo.
- **Lore:** (see companion notes)
- **Significance:** The snake represents the Drifter's philosophy: shedding skin, rebirth, survival. "Give someone something to chase and you give them purpose."

---

## The Seething Heart

- **What it is:** The heart of an Ascendant Primeval. Given to the Drifter by the Guardian.
- **Drifter's words:** "What. Is. This? The heart of an Ascendant Primeval. I'll take that, thank you very much. We'll use it later."
- **Significance:** Used in the creation of Malfeasance.

---

## The Stasis Prototype (Salvation's Grip)

- **What it is:** A Fallen weapon that uses Stasis (Darkness power).
- **Drifter's words:** "Let me have a look at that thing. I like that rough-around-the-edges look that Fallen tech has. Reminds me of the Dark Age. Smash together some bits and pieces, and pray to no one that it works. You could tear up the field in Gambit with this. Lay some traps. Catch some Taken."
- **Significance:** The Drifter sees Stasis as Dark Age tech returning. He's excited about it.

---

## Umbral Engrams

- **What it is:** Encrypted engrams that contain powerful gear.
- **Drifter's words:** "Well, that took you a while. You getting old? [laughs] I saw you snag that engram. You think no one was looking? You ready to get Dark? Chuck it on the Pyre and let's see what ol' Drifter can cook up for ya. Been kit-bashing gear since that jerk Ghost pulled me outta my grave. Got a knack for it, I guess. I'll work you up something nice."
- **Significance:** The Drifter can decrypt these. "Been kit-bashing gear since that jerk Ghost pulled me outta my grave."

## The Jade Coin (with Pyramid)

- **What it is:** The Drifter's coin, but with a triangular pyramid shape on it (shown in the Emissary's vision).
- **Significance:** The Nine are changing the game. The pyramid on the coin represents the coming Darkness — the Pyramids.

## The Ace of Spades (Cayde's Gun)

- **Type:** Hand Cannon (Kinetic)
- **Drifter's connection:** Cayde mentioned the Drifter in his stash messages. "This one's for the fellow calls himself The Drifter. You did warn me running with you would put a target on my back."
- **Significance:** The Drifter and Cayde had a partnership. Cayde's death hit the Drifter, even if he doesn't show it.

## The Haul (Detailed)

See "The Haul" above. Additional context:
- The Haul is a piece of the Nine's power — a fragment of their collective consciousness.
- It generates Motes of Dark, which are used in Gambit.
- The Drifter doesn't fully understand it: "I got a bad habit of biting off more'n I can chew."
- The Haul gives him visions: Visions of the Pyramids, the Nine's warnings, the coming Darkness.
- The Haul is connected to the Ascendant Plane and the Nine's realm.
