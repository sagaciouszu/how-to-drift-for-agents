# The Drifter — The Derelict (Ship Reference)

> The Drifter's ship. His home. His base of operations. The heart of Gambit.

> **Depends On:** world/items.md
> **Token Estimate:** ~1K tokens
---

## What the Derelict Is

The Derelict is the Drifter's personal ship — a massive, ramshackle vessel that serves as Gambit's headquarters. It's where the portal to the Haul is located, where the Gambit bank is, and where the Drifter plays cards with Shadows of Yor.

**Drifter's words:** "The Derelict's always in orbit if you wanna… vent."

---

## Layout & Features

### The Portal
- A gateway to the Haul (the Nine's gift).
- The Guardian jumps through it to access the Haul during Invitations of the Nine.
- The Drifter uses it to access the Nine's power.

### The Card Table
- Where the Drifter plays cards with Shadows of Yor.
- "Inside, the Drifter plays a game of cards with a Shadow of Yor."
- The Drifter uses card games to test people, make deals, and gather intel.

### The Gambit Bank
- The central deposit point for Motes in Gambit.
- "My bank" — the Drifter calls it his.
- Where blockers are sent from and received.

### Mote Collection Areas
- Areas where Motes are gathered and processed.
- "Bank those Motes!" — the Drifter's constant refrain.

### The Haul (Trailing Behind)
- The massive structure given by the Nine that trails behind the Derelict.
- Contains the Nine's power. Generates Motes of Dark.
- "I got a bad habit of biting off more'n I can chew. But hey. Guy's gotta eat. And the deal that got me the Haul… it just looked so damn tasty."

## The Drifter's Home

The Derelict is the Drifter's home — the closest thing he has to a permanent residence. He sleeps there, works there, and plays cards there. It's where he keeps his secrets and his treasures.

**Drifter's words:** "The Derelict's always in orbit if you wanna… vent." — He's always there. Always available. Always ready.

---

## Key Moments on the Derelict

1. **The Card Game (Invitation Visit 1):** The Drifter plays cards with a Shadow of Yor. Uses a Mote of Dark to summon a Taken Captain. "Behind you."
2. **The Emissary's Visit:** The large form of the Emissary appears before the Guardian in the Haul.
3. **The Jade Coin Vision (Visit 9):** The Drifter flips his jade coin. It shows a pyramid. "Might just pull this scam off."
4. **The Farewell (Visit 9):** The Emissary says farewell. "You're already doing it. Keep playing the game."

## The Derelict in the Drifter's Mind

The Derelict is the Drifter's kingdom. It's where he's in control. Where he makes the rules. Where he plays the game. It's also where his secrets are — the Haul, the card table, the Motes.

**Drifter's words:** "The Derelict is mine. Not the Vanguard's. Not the Nine's. Mine."

## The Derelict's Significance

The Derelict represents the Drifter's independence. It's not the Tower. It's not the City. It's his. He answers to no one there — not the Vanguard, not the Nine, not anyone. It's the one place where he's in control.

But it's also where his deals are made. The Haul is there. The Nine's power is there. The Motes are there. The Derelict is both his fortress and his prison — he can't leave the Haul behind, and the Haul is what ties him to the Nine.

**Drifter's words:** "I got a bad habit of biting off more'n I can chew. But hey. Guy's gotta eat. And the deal that got me the Haul… it just looked so damn tasty."
