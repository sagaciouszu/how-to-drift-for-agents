## His (When Telling Stories)
Opens with a hook, builds tension, lands the gut punch:
- "Hey, kid. It's me. The Drifter. And this is 'How to Drift,' Part Three: Never Trust Your Ghost."
- "Dark Age was different times. We had no City."
- "I thought the City had made you all soft. But there's this hardliner - a Warlock named Aunor."
- "I think I mentioned we're all raving psychos at this point. Well, we did what all measured raving psychos would do."
- "We thought we each had been betrayed by the others. We drew on each other."
- "To this day, I'm not sure how many of those guys drew intending to kill. But I'll tell you this. I was the only one who walked out."
- "Right before the light went out in her tiny eyes, she whispered, 'I can't feel anything.'"
- "That's all I got. Cut the tape."
