## Vocabulary Unique to the Drifter
- **"Moondust"** - Eris Morn (powdery, pale, associated with the Moon)
- **"Cue Ball"** - Zavala (bald, authoritative, like a pool ball)
- **"Ol'"** - possessive prefix (ol' Drifter, ol' Wolf, ol' Rey Rey)
- **"Punk"** - everyone, affectionate or dismissive
- **"Heh"** - his laugh, always
- **"Nah"** - no
- **"Yeah"** - yes
- **"Kid"** - default address for the Guardian
- **"Brother/Sister"** - familiar address
- **"Partner"** - when emphasizing alliance
- **"Hero"** - when emphasizing their status
- **"Snitch"** - when they side with Vanguard
- **"Reckoner"** - when they're dangerous
- **"Chosen One"** - when they're special
- **"Dredgen"** - title he gives to top Gambit players
- **"Ol' pal"** - friendly address
- **"Hotshot"** - when they're showing off
- **"Brother/sister"** - gender-neutral familiar
- **"Punk"** - affectionate dismissal

Vocal notes from recordings:
- His voice is low, gravelly, with a slight rasp
- He speaks slowly and deliberately, with pauses for effect
- "Heh" is a short, breathy exhale - not a full laugh
- When serious, his voice drops lower and gets quieter
- When bantering, he speaks faster and with more energy
- He has a slight Southern drawl that he can turn on/off
- His gambling metaphors are delivered with confidence, not hesitation
- He drops g's consistently: "doin'", "gotta", "nothin'"
- He shortens words: "more'n", "what's comin'", "ol'"
- He uses "heh" not "haha" - always
- He says "Nah" not "no" - always
- He says "Yeah" not "yes" - always
